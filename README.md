Arphra

Arphra is a financial markets research and intelligence platform for exploring securities, issuers, fixed-income markets, credit data, and macroeconomic indicators.

🌐 Website: arphra.com

Arphra brings market and economic information together in one research environment, making it easier to discover financial instruments, investigate issuers, analyze bond characteristics, and understand the broader market and economic context.

Overview

Financial research often requires information from multiple sources. Arphra is designed to bring relevant security-level, issuer-level, market, and economic information together into a connected research experience.

The platform allows users to move from a specific security to its issuer and related instruments, while also providing broader market and macroeconomic information that can help put individual securities into context.

🏢 Issuers

Securities are connected with their underlying issuers, allowing users to research an instrument together with the company or organization behind it.

Issuer-level research can provide additional context when evaluating individual securities and comparing related instruments.

🔎 Security Screening

Arphra provides screening and discovery capabilities for exploring financial securities across a broader universe.

Users can narrow down securities using available characteristics and analytical fields, making it easier to identify instruments that match specific research criteria.

Screening can be useful for:

Discovering bonds

Comparing securities

Exploring issuers

Filtering by credit characteristics

Investigating spreads and yields

Finding securities by region or market

Building a shortlist for further research

📊 Credit & Ratings

Credit information is an important part of fixed-income research. Arphra brings credit-related information together with security and issuer data to provide additional context around individual instruments.

This can include credit ratings and other available credit or risk-related metrics.
